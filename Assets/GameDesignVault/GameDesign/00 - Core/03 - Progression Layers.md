### Persistent collection

Things the player actually owns.

Potentially:

- characters
- weapons
- armour
- recipes
- augments/decorations
- cosmetics
- challenges
- boss discoveries

### Starting configuration

The subset deliberately brought into the run.

Potentially:

- character
- weapon
- armour
- augment slots
- guaranteed abilities
- objectives

### Run progression

Everything temporary.

- abilities discovered
- evolutions
- mutations
- incremental stat growth
- temporary effects
- synergies

### World state

A fourth progression axis people often forget.

- threat level
- boss schedule
- upsurges
- environmental effects
- Apex state
