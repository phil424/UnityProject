# Overview
Current broad concept:
> Modular modifications affecting a build.

May include behavioural and numerical effects.

Examples:
> **Volatile Wounds** - Bleeding enemies explode.

> **Attack Level I** - Increase weapon attack output according to weapon coefficients.

Don't finalise terminology yet.

# Compatibility
Potential compatibility:
- universal
- weapon
- ability
- projectile
- melee
- status
- character.

Rule:
> Restrictions should exist because the effect logically requires them, not merely to limit player creativity.
