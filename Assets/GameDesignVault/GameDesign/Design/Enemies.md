# Boss Design
Principles:
- boss should interact meaningfully with autonomous combat + ability intervention
- readable moments for player abilities
- worthwhile repeat hunting
- compatibility with multi-boss escalation
- not every boss mechanic can assume a private arena.

That last one is particularly important because bosses may eventually overlap.

# Reward Identity
For each important monster/boss answer:
> Why would I want to hunt this again?

Potential answers:
- crafting materials
- weapons
- armour
- ability unlocks
- augmentation pools
- cosmetics
- challenge rewards
- variants
- collection progress.
